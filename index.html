How many gates can you pass?

Help Pino the penguin race down an icy slope. Drag or tilt to slip between the flags, drift through the turns and launch off the ramps. Miss a gate and the run is over.

Easy to learn, hard to put down

Quick runs, one more go, and a high score that keeps calling you back.

Pass the gates

Go between each pair of flags to score. Pass a flag on the wrong side and the run is over.

Drag or tilt

Slide a finger left or right and Pino turns towards it. No finger on the screen? Tilt the phone to steer.

Drift

Turn the board sideways to scrub off speed and carve through tight turns. Straighten up to go fast again.

Jump ramps

Green ramps add points, red ones take them away. In the air you fly over the flags, so choose your landing.

Twist to brake

Rotate two fingers to swing the board fully sideways and stop hard, right before that tricky gate.

Top scores

Your best run goes to the Game Center and Google Play Games leaderboards. Share it and dare your friends to beat it.
